A prosecutor argues against the decision. A defender argues for it. The judge scores both and delivers a verdict that cites the transcript line by line. Recorded cases play with no key. A live trial needs a free Gemini key — yours, kept in your browser.

How a trial runs

  1. Three rounds, fixed order. Prosecutor, then defender, each round. The judge cross-examines after rounds two and three. No extra rounds, no drifting.
  2. Each side must steelman the other. Once a round, an advocate states the other side's strongest point fairly — then rebuts it. "I agree" is not allowed.
  3. The judge scores, not summarises. Evidence, logic, and risk, 0–10 each, shown live beside the transcript.
  4. The verdict cites its lines. Every verdict names the turns it relies on. Click a citation and the transcript jumps to the line. Object, or challenge the verdict, at any point.
